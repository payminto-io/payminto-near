Badge labels a thing with a short fixed word, such as a network or a role.

## Use
Use it for attributes that do not change with a Call.

## Provide
Provide a one or two word label and a `variant` of `default`, `secondary`, `destructive` or `outline`.

## Do and do not
Do use StatusPill for settled, waiting or refused.
Do not make a Badge look clickable unless it is a link.
