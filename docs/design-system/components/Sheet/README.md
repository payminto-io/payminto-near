Sheet slides a panel over the page, from the bottom on phones and the right on wider screens.

## Use
Use it for filters, details and mobile confirmations.
Use ApprovalSheet for approving a Call.

## Provide
Provide a title, content, and a close control of at least 44px.

## Rules
Options are at least 44px tall.
The footer is sticky and adds the bottom safe area.

## Do and do not
Do keep the deciding action in the footer.
Do not open a Sheet from a Sheet.
