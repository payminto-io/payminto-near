Timeline lists what happened to a Call, oldest first.

## Use
Use it on Call details and agent runs.

## Provide
Provide ordered events, each with a tone, lucide icon, title, time and optional detail.

## Rules
Dots are 19px with 12px icons and a 1px `border` connector.
Times are mono and exact.
End a refused Call on the Refusal with its reason.

## Do and do not
Do use the three status tones only for those meanings, and neutral or info otherwise.
Do not animate past events.
