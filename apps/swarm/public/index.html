How to read a bid

Sellers bid without seeing each other. First everyone publishes a hash, then everyone opens it.

Sealed

Only the hash of the bid is public. Nobody, the coordinator included, can read the price yet.

Revealed

The seller opened its bid on time and it matches its hash. It can win.

Void

Opened late, never opened, or not matching its hash. It cannot win and does not set the price.

Winner

The lowest valid bid. Every buyer pays the second-lowest valid price, per delivered unit.
